4Mobility, a digital car rental platform, transitioned from minute-based car-sharing to longer-term smart rentals and implemented automated KYC verification to combat fraud. The shift combined biometric and document validation through Authologic, significantly reducing fraudulent accounts and improving customer onboarding speed while maintaining security for their premium vehicle fleet.
