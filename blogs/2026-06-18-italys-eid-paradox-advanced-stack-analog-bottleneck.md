Italy missed its EU deadline to retire paper documents not because of bad technology but because of appointment slots. Despite leading Europe in eID adoption with 61 million electronic ID cards and 10 million IT Wallet activations, local registry offices lacked enough appointment availability to process remaining renewals before the August 3 deadline.
