A glossary explaining fundamental concepts in the EUDI ecosystem, framing how the EUDI wallet lets users log in without usernames and passwords, onboard without plastic documents, and authorise payments without SMS one-time passwords. Part one defines key terms including Wallet, PID (Personal Identity Credential), Relying Party, Relying Party Intermediary, and Verifier.
